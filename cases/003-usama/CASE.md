# CASE-003 — Usama b. Zayd expedition

**Status:** NOT STARTED — discovery only.

## Research question
What was the intended target and strategic purpose of the expedition associated with Usama b. Zayd, when was it ordered and executed, and what does its timing reveal—if anything—about policy at the end of Muhammad's life and the first caliphal administration?

## Separate questions
1. What do the earliest reports say about the order and target?
2. Did the expedition depart before Muhammad's death or under Abu Bakr?
3. How do different reports name the target (including Ubna) and describe the mission?
4. What is the relation, if any, between Usama's campaign and the Mu'ta/Mothous traditions?
5. Can operational decisions be separated from later legitimating narratives?

## Required cautions
Do not assume that later reports of a “second Mu'ta” mean the same military event or objective. Do not interpret continuity of command as proof of a single strategy without documentation.

## Next work
Collate source variants, isnads and chronological sequences; compare Theophanes, Syriac and Arabic material; assess whether the campaign was a punitive expedition, border operation or part of a wider northern policy.
