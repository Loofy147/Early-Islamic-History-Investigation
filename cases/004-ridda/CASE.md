# CASE-004 — Ridda campaigns and political reintegration

**Status:** NOT STARTED — discovery only.

## Research question
What political, fiscal, religious and tribal conflicts occurred in the regions later grouped under the label *Ridda*, and by what mechanisms did the Medina-based polity re-establish control?

## Decompose the label
Do not treat all regions or movements as one rebellion. Separate:
- refusal or dispute over tax/charitable obligations;
- withdrawal from political allegiance;
- rival claims to authority or prophecy;
- local succession and inter-tribal conflicts;
- attacks, counter-attacks and protection arrangements;
- negotiated submission versus military defeat.

## Questions
1. Which local actors and regions are independently identifiable?
2. What demands did Medina make, and how do sources preserve them?
3. When and how was force used?
4. What terms followed: taxation, hostage-taking, appointment, treaty or renewed allegiance?
5. How did later historiography organize diverse events into a unified narrative?

## Required cautions
Do not use “apostasy” as a neutral category without attributing it to sources or defining its legal/religious meaning. Do not presume every opponent had the same motive.

## Next work
Build a regional event register; compare early Islamic reports with epigraphy, documentary evidence and non-Muslim observations; map source dependence and later legal-theological framing.
