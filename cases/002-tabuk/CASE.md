# CASE-002 — Tabuk mobilization and northern Arabian frontier

**Status:** NOT STARTED — discovery only.  
**Traditional chronology:** 9 AH; exact conversion and source chronology to audit.

## Research question
What can be established about the mobilization conventionally called the expedition of Tabuk, its target, the presence or absence of an opposing force, its political purpose and the arrangements reached with northern communities?

## Separate questions
- Was the movement primarily a military deployment, coercive demonstration, intelligence operation, diplomatic mission or mixed action?
- What independent evidence exists for a planned Byzantine attack?
- Which actors were Byzantine officials, Arab allies, local rulers or communities?
- Which reported treaties or tribute arrangements can be independently verified?
- What was the actual operational outcome?

## Required cautions
Do not describe the operation as a battle unless a battle is evidenced. Do not infer that an unencountered enemy was either fictitious or imminent without testing source expectations. Do not collapse later treaty traditions into one original event.

## Next work
Identify earliest reports and their dates; compare Arabic sources with Greek/Syriac/epigraphic and documentary evidence; map routes, water and distances; register claims before synthesis.
