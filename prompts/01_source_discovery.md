# Source discovery prompt

Research question: [insert bounded question]

Find primary and secondary evidence in relevant languages and disciplines. Do not begin by accepting a preferred narrative.

## Requirements
1. Search bibliographies, critical editions, university catalogues, journal databases, digital manuscript repositories and documentary/epigraphic corpora.
2. Separate primary sources from modern scholarship and tertiary summaries.
3. For each result, provide full bibliographic metadata, language, genre, date of composition, event period, exact available locus and stable URL/DOI.
4. State whether the full text, a preview, abstract, catalogue record or snippet was actually inspected.
5. Identify possible dependence among sources; do not count translations or reprints as independent witnesses.
6. Search for contrary evidence, not only evidence supporting the leading explanation.
7. Record search terms, languages, repositories and coverage limits.
8. Do not invent unavailable pages, manuscript shelfmarks or exact quotations.

## Output
A candidate source list with source IDs, relevance rationale, access level, independence questions, and the next exact text to inspect. Do not make a historical conclusion from discovery results alone.
