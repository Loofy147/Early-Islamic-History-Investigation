# Claim extraction prompt

Input: [source text or precise extract]

Extract atomic claims without silently endorsing them.

For every claim:
- Assign a stable claim ID.
- Quote only the minimum necessary source wording and provide exact locus.
- Record the claim as a statement about what the source says.
- Identify separate event, chronology, actor, cause, force-composition and outcome claims.
- Record named actors, place names and dates exactly as transmitted.
- Mark ambiguities, editorial additions, translation choices and variant readings.
- Distinguish explicit text from inference.
- Link source IDs and any contrary source IDs.
- Suggest at least one alternative explanation.
- Assign a claim state and confidence rationale only where justified.

Do not convert a source report into a historical fact. Do not assign a confidence level without explaining the evidence and dependence.
