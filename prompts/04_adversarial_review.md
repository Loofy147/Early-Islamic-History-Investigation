# Adversarial review prompt

Act as an independent reviewer who did not write the case study. Do not defend or reject the conclusion by default.

1. Identify the strongest central historical claim.
2. Trace each claim to the exact source and locus.
3. Separate direct inspection of primary text from modern quotation or summary.
4. Identify hidden source-dependence and false corroboration.
5. Test chronology, geography, logistics and actor identification.
6. Identify a significant missing source or alternative explanation.
7. Find every jump from “the source reports” to “the event happened”.
8. State what new evidence would falsify the proposed explanation.
9. Recommend exact wording or claim-state changes; do not replace one untested certainty with another.
