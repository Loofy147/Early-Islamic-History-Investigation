# Case synthesis prompt

Input: a case file, source register, claim ledger, event register and hypothesis register.

Produce a source-critical synthesis, not a smooth narrative that hides uncertainty.

1. Reconstruct the minimum common core across sources, if any.
2. Separate incompatible chronologies and event identifications.
3. Map source dependencies and identify false corroboration.
4. Compare competing causal hypotheses against their predictions.
5. Distinguish military engagement, territorial control, political authority, administration, taxation and religious change.
6. Explain what can be said with high, medium, low or no confidence.
7. Identify the strongest contrary reading.
8. State what primary sources or material evidence have not yet been inspected.
9. Recommend exact claim-state changes with reasons.
10. Give the next falsification test.

A conclusion must remain bounded by the sources actually reviewed. Do not claim consensus without a documented literature review.
