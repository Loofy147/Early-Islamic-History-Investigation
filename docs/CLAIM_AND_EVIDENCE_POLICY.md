# Claim and evidence policy

## Claim states
| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| FACT | A specific fact supported by strong direct and/or converging evidence. |
| ESTABLISHED | A relatively stable conclusion after source criticism and alternatives. |
| REPORTED | A source reports this; the project does not thereby endorse it. |
| INFERENCE | An analytical conclusion drawn from recorded evidence. |
| HYPOTHESIS | A testable explanation. |
| DISPUTED | Material sources or interpretations conflict. |
| UNKNOWN | Available evidence does not permit an answer. |
| CONTRADICTED | Stronger evidence conflicts with the claim as presently worded. |

## Required claim record
Every material claim needs a stable ID, atomic wording, state, case ID, supporting and contrary source IDs, exact locus, accurate evidence summary, alternative explanation, confidence rationale and review history.

## Anti-inflation rules
- A translation is not a second witness to its source text.
- A modern quotation of an ancient source is not an additional ancient witness.
- Silence is not contradiction unless mention would be expected under a justified model.
- Do not infer prior intention from a later outcome.
- Do not infer causation from chronology alone.
- Repetition without independent evidence does not raise confidence.
- Distinguish the truth of “a source says X” from the truth of X.
