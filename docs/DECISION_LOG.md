# Research decision log

## DEC-0001 — Do not fix the Mu'ta date yet
**Date:** 2026-11-20  
**Decision:** Do not register 8 AH / 629 CE as an established date. Record it as the conventional chronology in the Islamic narrative tradition.  
**Reason:** Powers notes that al-Waqidi's narrative placement implies year 8 AH without explicitly dating the battle in its own report; Ibn Ishaq material through Ibn Hisham gives a dated notice according to Powers.  
**Review trigger:** Collate the Arabic texts, isnads and variants.

## DEC-0002 — Do not label the opposing force simply "the Byzantine army"
**Date:** 2026-11-20  
**Decision:** Keep force identity UNKNOWN; use precise language for local/regional/imperial actors only when source-supported.  
**Reason:** Sources and modern reconstructions differ about regular imperial troops versus local Arab groups and regional forces.  
**Review trigger:** Audit Byzantine administration, garrisons, local allies and primary-text wording.

## DEC-0003 — Do not treat Theophanes' Mothous notice as confirmed Mu'ta
**Date:** 2026-11-20  
**Decision:** Separate the Theophanes engagement at Mothous from the Islamic narrative of Mu'ta until geographical and textual identity is established.  
**Reason:** The translation's note on p.466 says the identification is unclear and records proposals of Ma'ab, Khirbat al-Mahna and Mu'an. Theophanes' entry is under AM 6123 [AD 630/1], and its chronology contains a tension between the statement that Muhammad had died earlier and the note identifying his death as 632.  
**Effect:** Claim CLM-MUT-005 describes Theophanes' Mothous notice only; identity with Mu'ta is a separate DISPUTED claim.  
**Review trigger:** Inspect Greek critical edition, apparatus, place-name variants and independent topographic arguments.
