# Terminology policy

Use descriptive terms such as campaign, engagement, raid, military deployment, sovereignty transfer, treaty, negotiated surrender, administrative incorporation, fiscal reorganization and occupation only where operationally defined.

Use “conquest”, “opening”, “invasion” and “occupation” when:
1. quoting or attributing a source or scholar; or
2. explicitly analyzing a defined term; or
3. applying transparent criteria supported by evidence.

Do not let these terms act as preset moral verdicts. Distinguish:
- religious conversion/spread;
- political sovereignty;
- military control;
- administrative and fiscal control;
- migration and demographic change.

Name actors precisely: emperor, governor, garrison commander, local coalition, tribe, army, state or religious community. Do not use “Byzantines”, “Arabs” or “Muslims” as a single undifferentiated actor when the evidence permits greater precision.
