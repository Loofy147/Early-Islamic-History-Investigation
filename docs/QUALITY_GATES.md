# Quality gates

A claim or case cannot be marked established or closed until the relevant gates are passed.

## G1 — Question and scope
The question, date range, geography, actors and key terms are explicit.

## G2 — Source provenance
The actual edition, translation or artifact is identified; exact locus and access status are recorded.

## G3 — Source criticism
Composition date, transmission, proximity, purpose, bias, dependence and limits have been assessed.

## G4 — Claim decomposition
Composite statements are split into atomic source-content, event, chronology, causal and outcome claims.

## G5 — Independence
Apparent corroboration is checked for shared sources, intermediaries and common traditions.

## G6 — Triangulation
Narrative evidence is compared with relevant documentary, material, geographic or logistical evidence where available.

## G7 — Adversarial alternatives
At least one strong rival explanation is specified, and the strongest contrary evidence is recorded.

## G8 — Uncertainty and reproducibility
The state, confidence rationale, open questions and exact sources inspected are recorded; another researcher can reproduce the inference.

## Closure states
- OPEN: work remains or evidence is insufficient.
- PROVISIONAL: current synthesis is useful but one or more material gates remain.
- CLOSED-BOUNDED: answer is bounded by available evidence and remaining uncertainties are explicit.
- REOPENED: new evidence or a reproducible contradiction invalidates a prior closure.

A passed structural validator is not a passed historical quality gate.
