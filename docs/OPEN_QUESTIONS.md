# Open questions — Mu'ta / Mothous

## Priority 0 — event identity and chronology
1. Does Mothous identify Mu'ta, or is it another locality? The translation's note explicitly calls identification unclear and lists Ma'ab, Khirbat al-Mahna and Mu'an.
2. Are Mouchea and Mothous distinct places in the Greek syntax and geography?
3. What are the Greek forms and manuscript variants of both toponyms?
4. How do AM 6122–6125 headings and imperial/episcopal synchronisms work?
5. Why does AM 6123 [AD 630/1] say Muhammad had died earlier while the editorial note gives his death as 632?
6. Why is Aboubacharos in his first year under AM 6123, second under AM 6124 and third under AM 6125? What chronology did Theophanes' source use?

## Priority 1 — Arabic chronology
7. What is the earliest verifiable Arabic locus for Jumada I, year 8 AH?
8. Does al-Waqidi state the date directly, or does his arrangement merely imply it?
9. What do variants and isnads in Ibn Ishaq/Ibn Hisham, Ibn Sa'd and al-Tabari show?

## Priority 2 — source dependence
10. What is Conrad's full argument about Theophanes and Arabic historical transmission?
11. What does Hoyland's work on circulation of historical knowledge add?
12. Is there an independent Greek/Syriac stream, or is the Theophanes passage derived from eastern/Arabic material?

## Priority 3 — military and geographic setting
13. What Byzantine administrative or military presence existed in the Balqa/Transjordan in the relevant year(s)?
14. What changed after Sasanian withdrawal from Syria/Palestine?
15. Do routes, water sources, distances and troop claims fit the proposed event site(s)?
16. Does Knauf's reconstruction still hold if Mothous cannot be securely identified with Mu'ta?

## Priority 4 — causes and outcomes
17. Can the envoy's mission and killing be verified outside the same narrative tradition?
18. What authority did Shurahbil b. Amr represent?
19. Was the force aimed at punishment, deterrence, a local political network or wider influence?
20. Is there evidence of transfer of sovereignty, taxation or administrative arrangements directly after the encounter?

## Rule
An unanswered or unsearched question remains UNKNOWN. Failure to find evidence in a limited search is not proof of non-existence.
